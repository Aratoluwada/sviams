SVIAMS: A Handheld Automated Gate Verification and Vehicle Inspection System Combining Plate Recognition and RFID Card Verification

Abubakar S.O, et al
Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta.

Abstract

Keywords: RFID, Automated gate, Vehicle inspection, Plate Recognition, embedded systems

Introduction
1.1
In the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta, there was a need to identify and tag each privately-owned vehicle that went into the campus every day. So the management decided to implement a manual system of handing out plastic tags and manually filling out the tag number against the plate number of the vehicle in a paperback book. But there were problems with that system. It was painfully slow, there was no secure database, information recorded could not be sourced and accessed easily. So we decided to build the SVIAMS.
This paper presents SVIAMS, a handheld unit built on a Raspberry Pi 4 that checks both at once. At entry it binds a registered RFID card to the plate read at the gate. At exit it reads the card and the plate again and reports whether they match. Every check is logged in a local database that an administrator can review.
The contributions are:

1. A portable, battery-powered gate checker that pairs a plate read with an RFID card read.
2. A plate recognition model trained on real plates from the FUNAAB gate and a strict Nigerian plate format.
3. An evaluation of the model on a cleaned test set and on fresh gate plates captured with the device's own camera. The second result is still to come.
